# LED-173 · Home Upcoming Bills includes overdue installments — retro (2026-09-27)

## What shipped
- `buildUpcomingLoanBills` moved from `useDashboardData.ts` into `src/lib/loanInstallments.ts` (it had no React dependency, so it's now covered by `node --test` instead of only by a live check).
- The per-installment cycle check no longer excludes a date before today (`floor`); only the cycle's end still bounds it, so an overdue installment stays on the strip instead of vanishing.
- Every purchase's bill for a loan account now carries the same `payment` (amount + date) as `nextDeadlineInCycle(getLoanDeadlines(...))` — the same builder `RepaymentAssist` and `accountsOverview.ts`'s `comingUp` use — so "Pay now" always opens the form on the figure it will itself display as "Installment due," regardless of which of the account's bills was clicked.
- `DashboardUpcomingBillsCard`: a negative `daysUntil` now renders "N days overdue" instead of "in -N days"; its color uses the overdue/urgent tone (`EXPENSE`) for `daysUntil <= 0`, not just `=== 0`.

## Acceptance (live re-run: headless Chrome over CDP, seeded user — Phone Loan with a Phone purchase 11 days overdue and a Sofa purchase due in 2 days; Car Loan with a Car purchase 7 days overdue and a Furniture purchase 2 days overdue)
- (a) overdue installments are listed, overdue first: **PASS, live**. Home's Upcoming Bills read, in order: Phone $250.00 "11 days overdue", Car $250.00 "7 days overdue", Furniture $100.00 "2 days overdue", Sofa $100.00 "in 2 days" — overdue rows first, most overdue first, exactly the fixture's own numbers (using 2 days instead of the ticket's 4, chosen so the date stayed inside September's cycle). Also PASS via `tests/loanInstallments.test.mjs`.
- (b) Pay now on the overdue row opens the form with the matching amount and "Installment due" line: **PASS, live**. Pay now on Phone opened "Record loan repayment" with Amount `250`, "Outstanding $350.00", "Installment due $250.00, Sep 16 · 11 days overdue" — the amount field and the stat line agree.
- (c) Accounts and Home list the same items for the same data: **confirmed as PARTIAL, not a test artifact**. Live, Accounts' Coming up showed exactly two rows — "Phone Loan installment $250.00 11d overdue" and "Car Loan installment $250.00 7d overdue" (one per account, earliest deadline only) — while Home showed all four purchase-level bills. Read literally ("same items"), the counts differ by design: Accounts is one row per account, Home is one row per purchase. What is guaranteed identical is the *figure* — Pay now on Home's non-overdue Sofa row also opened the form at amount 250 (the account's true next deadline, same as the overdue row and the same as what Accounts shows for that account), so the two surfaces never disagree on what is actually due, even though Home shows more rows. See Backlog.
- (d) unit test on the shared builder: PASS — `tests/loanInstallments.test.mjs`, five cases including the pay-period branch and a fully-paid installment.
