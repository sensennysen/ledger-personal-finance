# Epics 15-19 · Phase 1 — Trust the numbers — retro (2026-09-29)

Branch `epics-15-19-phase-1`, off `redesign-m3` (task CSVs cherry-picked from `epics-15-19-planning`, which those docs lived on and which predates the epic-14 merge). Five tickets, five commits: LED-182, 183, 184, 186, 187.

## What was built
- `src/lib/convertedTotals.ts` (`sumConverted`): the one pure helper LED-182 asked for, wrapping `amountInCurrency` the way `summarizeBalances` already does for account balances. `useDashboardData.ts`'s `sumTransactionsByType` (deleted) and its per-period cash-flow loop now read it; Home's income/expense/net stat cards and cash-flow chart show a new `UnratedCurrencyNotice` for whatever it excludes.
- `buildCategoryBreakdown` (`categoryBreakdown.ts`), `groupExpensesByCategory` (`utils.ts`) and `summarizeRange` (`periodCompare.ts`) all took a `target`/`table` pair and switched their `t.amount * (t.exchange_rate ?? 1)` (or plain `+= t.amount`) to `amountInCurrency`, returning the currencies they had to leave out alongside their existing totals. `FilterTopCategories` dropped its "whichever currency has the most rows wins" workaround for one combined, converted list.
- Found and fixed alongside LED-183 (same file, same bug, not its own ticket): `ReportsPage`'s "Income vs. Expenses" trend chart (`monthlyData`) had the identical `t.amount * (t.exchange_rate ?? 1)` line as `summarizeRange`. Now reuses `summarizeRange` per lookback bucket.
- `convertedNetWorthEffect` (`periodCompare.ts`), new and separate from `netWorthEffect`: the net-worth walk-back (LED-184) needed conversion, the "Net Change" stat card (same file, un-ticketed) did not get touched, so `netWorthEffect` itself is untouched and its existing callers are unaffected.
- LED-186 turned out not to be a "fix the rate" ticket: the deletion-export budgets CSV had **no Spent column at all** (checked against the shipped LED-143 code, not just the LED-136 retro's description of it). Added `Spent` and `Unrated Currencies` columns. Active budgets already had a correctly converted `.spent` from `useBudgets()` (LED-136 wired that up); inactive ones needed their own minimal read (`useInactiveBudgetsForExport`, new, in `useBudgets.ts` but not touching the existing 50-degree `useBudgets` function), reusing `sumBudgetSpend` and the transactions the export card already loads rather than a second paginated fetch.
- LED-187: `rateStatusLabel` (`exchangeRates.ts`) checks `neededCount === 0` before it will ever say "fetched", so a stale `fetchedAt` from a since-removed foreign account can't make the status lie.
