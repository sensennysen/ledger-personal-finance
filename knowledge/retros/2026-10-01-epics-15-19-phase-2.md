## Backlog
- **LED-192 from the real global modal.** The modal's own path (`useCardPayment` in AppLayout calling `notifyCardPaymentsRefresh`) was not driven: the Change kind control did not respond to scripted clicks. The page side and the notify call are each checked; the join is by inspection.
- **Fresh-database replay** of the new migration was not run locally (`pnpm db:reset` would wipe the local data). The CI `db` job does it.
- **Drain then close.** A queued card payment's statement step runs in the tab that drains it. If the tab closes between the insert and the step, the payment is saved and its statement is not updated, and no Fix notification appears.
- **A capped paid amount is lossy.** When a payment pushed the paid amount past the statement balance, editing or deleting it subtracts the full amount, so paid can end lower than the sum of the remaining payments. Seen in psql; not corrected.
- **Unlinked older payments.** Payments with no matching transfer (or an ambiguous match) are not linked, so editing their transfer leaves the statement alone.
- **Export column.** `transaction_id` is not in the credit-card-payments export; LED-180 owns the export wording.
- **Delete of a queued create** still queues a delete for a row the database has not seen (only the edit was fixed).
- **Category filter counts** in the filter UI were checked only through the top categories rail and Categories page.
- **Design handoff copy.** `Ledger - 2B Screens.dc.html` still says "expense against the card"; the handoff was not edited.
