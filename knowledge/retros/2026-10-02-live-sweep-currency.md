## Method
`knowledge/patterns/live-sweep-method.md` and `browser-check-with-local-user.md`. Local Supabase only (`pnpm db:reset`, then `supabase/seed.sql`); Vite dev server; headless Chromium driven by Playwright. No live account was touched.

- **Sweep user** `sweep@ledger.local`, inserted with psql like the seed (pattern item 25): default currency PHP, BPI Checking (PHP), GCash (PHP), BDO Mastercard (PHP), USD Savings (USD) and EUR Wallet (EUR), one income and five expenses this month (PHP 4,700, USD 65, EUR 60). It is deleted at the end of the phase.
- **The real feed.** `api.frankfurter.dev` was allowed in this environment's network policy for the sweep. Chromium does not trust the egress proxy's CA, so the driver answers the browser's feed request with Node's own `fetch` through the proxy (`NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`) and passes the host's status and body on unchanged. The app fetched `https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/rates?base=PHP&quotes=EUR%2CUSD` and got HTTP 200, `[{"date":"2026-10-02","base":"PHP","quote":"EUR","rate":0.01414},{"date":"2026-10-02","base":"PHP","quote":"USD","rate":0.01596}]`.
- **Blocked feed** = the driver aborts the request (`internetdisconnected`), which the app sees as a failed fetch.
- **Time** was moved by editing `exchange_rates.fetched_at` with psql, not the clock.
- **Offline** was checked twice: once with the `navigator.onLine` stub (pattern item 26) and once with a real network cut (`context.setOffline(true)`), without reloading (item 15).
