## What to change
- **A FAIL noted in a retro was never filed.** LED-228's 3.55:1 tab label was measured by LED-116 and named as needing a ticket by LED-139, LED-152 and the phase 9 retro. It got a ticket only now. `live-sweep-method.md` item 5 now says so.
- **The contrast walker has blind spots.** It reads a fill without its opacity, so the 10% circles on the Reports stat cards flag as light surfaces. It skips SVG text, and it does not see focus rings, which LED-229 had to measure separately. Added to `rendered-contrast-scan.md`.
- **The sweep data lived only in a container.** The sweep users (`sweep@ledger.local`, `empty@ledger.local`'s 14 categories) and the edited demo rows were in the local Supabase of the session's container. Its Docker daemon is no longer running, so that database is gone rather than reset by hand. Nothing reached the linked remote. A fresh `pnpm db:reset` gives a clean seed.
