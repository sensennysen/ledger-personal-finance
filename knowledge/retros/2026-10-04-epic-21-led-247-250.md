## What went well
- **Reading the built CSS caught a trap the tests could not.** For browsers without `color-mix`, Tailwind falls back to the mix's first colour. `color-mix(in srgb, var(--expense) 12%, …)` therefore fell back to the label's own colour. Swapping the operands keeps the same colour and makes the fallback safe. This is now `patterns/theme-token-contrast.md` item 7.
- **Calibrating the CDP sweep against the old bug** before trusting its zero.

## What to change
- **Playwright is not installed on this machine, so `pnpm sweep` did not run.** The fallback (CDP plus the sweep's own page functions) is now in `patterns/rendered-contrast-scan.md`. The sweep could fall back to CDP itself instead of exiting.
- **Toggling `.dark` by hand needs a wait.** `transition-colors` made a scan read 1.06:1 mid-fade.
