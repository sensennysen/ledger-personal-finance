## What went well
- **Forcing the failure proved the atomic claim.** A temporary trigger that raised at the budgets step showed the source, its transactions and its subcategories untouched. A refusal before the first write would not have shown that.
- **Old duplicates were planned for, and that found a bug.** LED-233 keeps existing duplicates. Thinking about what that meant for LED-239 showed a set-based subcategory move would trip the new trigger, so the merge loops and folds them.
- **One Reorder pattern, twice.** Categories and subcategories now share `ReorderButtons` and `refocusMove`. The width-switched drag/arrow pair is gone from both.

## What to change
- **`db diff` replays migrations without touching local data.** Use it for "applies to an empty database" instead of `db reset`. Seed problems still need a read of `seed.sql`, or CI's `db` job.
- **A local stack can lag the branch.** Three 2026-10-03 migrations were missing locally. Run `pnpm db:migrate` at the start of a phase.
