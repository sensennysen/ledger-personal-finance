# Epic 22 · Phase 3 — Feature gaps — retro (2026-10-06)

Branch `epic-22`. Four tickets: LED-270, LED-272, LED-271 and LED-269. Three feature commits. LED-272 needed no change. Owner decisions taken in /plan: LED-270 routes imported card payments through the card path instead of dropping cards from the picker, and LED-271 shows no streak for weekly and quarterly rows instead of counting one in their own periods.

## What was built
- **LED-270** (`2f8a6d0`).
  - **Before:** an imported transfer into a credit card was a plain insert. The card balance moved, but no `credit_card_payments` row was written and the statement's paid amount stayed put. The card-payment triggers fire on UPDATE and DELETE only. A queued (offline) import already got the statement steps on drain (`recordSynced`), so online and offline imports disagreed.
  - **Now:** `bulkCreateTransactions(rows, onCardPayment)` returns the saved rows. Each transfer into a card, oldest first and awaited, goes to `recordGenerated`, the same statement steps a recurring payment runs (`importedCardPayments` in `importTransfer.ts`).
- **LED-272** (no commit). `payNowDate` (LED-195, `loanInstallments.ts`) already opens an overdue bill on today and keeps an upcoming bill on its due date, for all three Pay now prefills. LED-195 decided the upcoming-bill question, and its retro (2026-10-01) has the live PASS.
- **LED-271** (`9b23789`).
  - Non-monthly budgets are saved with `streak: 0`; only monthly budgets are walked cycle by cycle.
  - `overspendingWindow(period)` gives weekly rows "weekly budget" / "This week" and quarterly rows "quarterly budget" / "This quarter". Yearly keeps "year to date" / "This year".
  - `spendWindowLabel` is unchanged, because Budgets also uses it.
- **LED-269** (`e431d08`).
  - **Migration `20261006130000`:** a loan repayment is credited, reversed and split (`allocate_loan_payment`) by `coalesce(destination_amount, amount)`. An edit to `destination_amount` alone re-splits it.
  - **Client mirrors:** `paymentCredit` and `needsAmountReceived` (`transferCredit.ts`) feed the optimistic deltas, the running balance and row amounts.
  - **Card and loan forms:** Pay from offers any currency, same currency first (`defaultPaymentSource`). "Amount received (CUR)" shows for card and loan payments. The card presets and RepaymentAssist fill that field and suggest the amount sent from the rate feed. The outstanding check uses the received amount. `cardPaymentTransfer` keeps `destination_amount`.
  - **Import:** transfer targets in any currency (no loans). A cross-currency row shows "Received (CUR)" or "Sent (CUR)", prefilled from the feed, and import is blocked while it is empty (`importTransferAmounts`).
  - **Rule:** `card-payment-is-a-transfer.md` gained item 7.
