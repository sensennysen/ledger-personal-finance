## What was built
- **LED-273** (`e3d0101`).
  - **Palette:** the plan's cause (a controlled `value`) was wrong; an uncontrolled try still showed no `aria-activedescendant` on open. cmdk 1.1.1's scheduler drops work queued during its own flush, including the `selectedItemId` step after the first auto-highlight. `patches/cmdk@1.1.1.patch` swaps the queue before running it. Pattern: `cmdk-drops-work-scheduled-in-its-flush.md`.
  - **Activity search:** `aria-label` "Search transactions" / "Search this cycle" beside the placeholder.
  - **Queue sheet:** "Nothing is waiting." is a `role="status"` paragraph mounted with the sheet (empty while items remain), so discarding the last item is announced. The list is `hidden` when empty, and the status takes `flex-1`, so the footer stays at the bottom.
- **LED-275** (`c1371e0`). New `--skeleton` token: light #C5C3CB, dark `var(--muted)`. `Skeleton` and `SkeletonText` use `bg-skeleton`. `themeContrast.test.mjs` asserts ≥1.5:1 on `--card` and `--sidebar` in light, the dark alias, and the component class. No raw `bg-muted animate-pulse` bypasses `Skeleton`.
- **LED-274** (`0a93a0d`). Home's docked detail pane is `w-[340px] 2xl:w-[400px]`. The account side column is unchanged (below).
- **LED-276** (`98657a5`). `public/social-preview.svg` redrawn in the dark tokens with the `LedgerMark` outline, the wordmark, a tagline and a schematic Net worth card (no real data). Rendered to PNG with headless Chrome through an HTML wrapper that loads Roboto and DM Mono, `--window-size=1200,630 --virtual-time-budget=5000`. index.html is unchanged.
