# Epic 23 · Phase 2 — Currency and import gaps — retro (2026-10-06)

Branch `epic-23`, not pushed since phase 1. Six tickets: LED-292, LED-293, LED-281, LED-282, LED-283 and LED-284, one commit each, plus one LED-293 fix found in validation. Client only: no migrations.

## What was built
- **LED-292** (`c52b597`).
  - **Home:** in the current cycle, `buildUpcomingLoanBills` takes the first unpaid deadline or installment due by the cycle's end, in both the pay-period and per-purchase branches. An installment overdue from an earlier cycle is now the bill: it shows "21 days overdue" in the expense colour, and Pay now opens on today (`payNowDate`). A past or future cycle keeps the in-cycle bound.
  - **Loan page:** `handleRecordPayment` dates a past deadline on today through `payNowDate`. Both Make payment buttons and the per-deadline Record payment use it.
- **LED-293** (`d43519b`, `8a83206`).
  - `paySourceAmounts` (`transferCredit.ts`, pure). A preset is what the target receives. On a same-currency Pay from it is the amount. From another currency it is the amount received, and the amount sent is converted from the rate feed, or left empty with no rate.
  - The form keeps `presetReceived` (set by the card presets, by `RepaymentAssist`'s new `onPreset`, and by a prefilled amount) and `sentTyped`. Typing in Amount or Received clears the preset. A preset clicked with a foreign Pay from replaces an amount sent the user did not type.
- **LED-281** (`00741fe`). The compact row's currency chip printed `tx.currency`; it now prints `amountDisplay`'s currency, as the amount and the list variant already did.
- **LED-282** (`e1b1d38`). `netByCurrency` (`transactionWindow.ts`): outside an account a transfer opens no currency. `sumByCurrency`, the day headers (`groupByDay`) and Month jump (`buildMonthNets`) share it. A day or month that is only transfers reads a zero in the first row's currency rather than a blank. Inside an account nothing changes.
- **LED-283** (`8396179`).
  - **Beside Import:** a `role="status"` line, "N rows need an amount received or sent".
  - **On the row:** the empty field points to its error ("Enter the amount received" or "sent") through `aria-describedby`.
- **LED-284** (`3c8c41b`). `OverspendingRow.window` holds the uncut `rangeFor` range for weekly, quarterly and yearly rows. `overspendingWindow(period, window, today)` keeps "This week", "This quarter" or "This year" while the window holds today. Otherwise it names the window: "Aug 31 to Sep 6", "Sep 28 to Oct 4", "Q3 2026", "2025".

## Findings
- **[FIX NOW, fixed in `8a83206`] LED-293 failed from Pay now.**
  - **Cause:** the first build recorded a preset only when a preset button ran. Pay now, Make payment and Pay card all open the form with the amount in `defaultValues`, so a switch still kept 456 as EUR. That is the exact path in the bug report.
  - **Fix:** a new liability payment opened with an amount starts from it as the preset. The switch only applies with a card or loan chosen (`paymentTarget`).
  - Pattern: `prefill-is-an-entry-point.md`.

- **LED-284:** a past yearly row still says "year to date" after its spent figure; it should say the year covered. The chip is right.
- **LED-282 (b):** not seen live (unit only).

- **LED-293 edge:** after a manual edit of the amount sent, a later switch keeps the typed figure as the new source's currency. That is the old behaviour, and the user's figure wins by design.
