How Ledger handles your data
Updated Sep 1, 2026
Ledger stores your transactions, accounts and budgets so that they are available across your devices. Entries created offline stay on your device until the app can reach the server.
What we store
Account identity — your email address and display name.
Financial records you enter yourself — we never connect to a bank.
Preferences — theme, text size, cycle start day.
Deleting your data
You can remove everything from Settings. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Notes for implementation
Nothing new was invented. Every screen here is assembled from patterns already specified: the 40px container-tile list row, the card header, the segmented button, the chip row, the 340px detail pane, the M3 drawer pill. No new tokens, no new type sizes.
Reports is the one screen with new structure — a 1.6 / 1 split with the bar chart left and category ranking plus an insight card right, four stat cards above. Recharts series map to --chart-2 / --chart-3; the saved line is --chart-1. The category ranking is horizontal bars, not a pie — it ranks and shows share in one read, and it survives a 300px column.
Desktop keeps every destination in the drawer. The four-item bottom nav is a mobile constraint; the drawer has room for all seven, so Sidebar.tsx is unchanged by the nav rework.
13th month is a calculator, not a report — the result leads in a filled primary-container hero, inputs sit below it, and the PD 851 caveat is a transfer-container note rather than fine print.
