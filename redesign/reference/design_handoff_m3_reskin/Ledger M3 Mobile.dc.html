iOS-corrected deltas
One M3 system, adapted where iOS forces our hand
Ledger ships as a single PWA, so the design system stays Material 3 on both platforms. The four deltas below are the only design differences — nothing is restyled for its own sake. Frame content is abridged to fit the shorter iOS canvas, so read the baseline opposite for the full screen.
1 · Safe-area inset
Nav bar grows to 114px (80 + 34pt env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); FAB lifts to clear the home indicator. Needs viewport-fit=cover.
2 · In-app back, always
No browser chrome in standalone, and the edge swipe dead-ends at the root. Every detail route leads with back + breadcrumb.
3 · Draft survives exit
A home swipe can suspend or kill the app mid-entry. The sheet autosaves and says so, rather than trusting the session.
4 · Manual install path
beforeinstallprompt never fires on iOS, so the Share → Add to Home Screen hint is the only route.
