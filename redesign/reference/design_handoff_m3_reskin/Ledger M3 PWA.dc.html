What changed vs. the re-skin
bottom_navigation
Four real destinations, no “More” tab
Home · Accounts · Activity · Budgets. /settings was a nav destination pretending to be a menu; it now lives behind the avatar with Reports, Categories and 13th Month, so every bar item is a place you can be.
add_circle
FAB out of the bar, amount-first sheet
The centre notch ate a nav slot and put the primary action where thumbs stretch. It moves to the M3 bottom-end FAB and opens a keypad sheet: type, amount, category — three taps to logged.
event_available
Month cycle is global, not per-page
The stepper sits under the app bar on every destination, so “which month am I looking at” is never a question — and Activity, Budgets and Home stay in sync.
install_mobile
PWA state is first-class
The install prompt and offline banner stop being stray banners: offline pins to the top with a pending-sync count, install floats above the FAB and dismisses for good. Toggle both from the avatar sheet or the Tweaks panel.
swipe_up
Sheets, never dialogs
Add, account menu and transaction detail are all thumb-reachable bottom sheets with drag handles — one interaction model instead of a popover, a dialog and a page.
Repo impact: BottomNav.tsx loses its More item and centre FAB, AppLayout.tsx gains the cycle bar + a single sheet host, and /budgets is promoted from the More menu to a route in the bar. The token layer is unchanged from the re-skin.
